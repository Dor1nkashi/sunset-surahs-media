# Sunset Surahs Poster: Privacy Policy

Sunset Surahs Poster is a private script used only by the owner of the Sunset Surahs channel (Umar Ahmed Jahangir) to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

- It does not collect, store or share data about any other person.
- The only Google data it uses is the permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel (youtube.upload scope).
- Access tokens are kept only on the owner's own computer and are never sent anywhere except to Google and Meta when posting.
- Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
